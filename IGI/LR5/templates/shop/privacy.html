1. Terms

Personal data
Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable person, such as a name, phone number or e-mail address.
Account
Your registered profile on this site, used to place orders and to leave reviews.
Order
A purchase made through the cart, together with its delivery date, pickup point and promo code.

2. Data we collect

Categories of personal data and the purposes of their use
Category Examples Purpose
Account Username, first and last name, password (stored in hashed form) Sign-in and identification
Contact details Phone number, e-mail address, delivery address, date of birth Order processing, delivery, age check
Orders Products, quantities, delivery date, pickup point, promo code Order fulfilment, purchase history, statistics
Reviews Author name, rating, review text Publication on the Reviews page
Payment card details are not collected or stored by this site.

3. Cookies

The site uses only technical cookies that are required for it to work:

sessionid
Keeps you signed in and remembers the contents of your cart.
csrftoken
Protects forms from forged requests.
Can I disable cookies?

Yes, in your browser settings. Please note that without these cookies you will not be able to sign in or use the cart.

  3. ask us to delete your data;
  4. withdraw your consent to the processing of your data.

5. Contact us

To exercise your rights, use the details on the Contacts page and use the subject Personal data request.
